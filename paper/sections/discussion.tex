\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{What we do not claim.}
We do not claim that VLMs over-trust degraded images in general. The MI is
undefined in most real conditions, so our VLM results rest on terminal
reliance and utility. The data are one dataset and one answer format; the
fusion study is a two-view proxy; and no driving evaluation is reported.

\paragraph{What the measures reveal.}
The pairing of utility and reliance along a severity path exposes
behaviour that accuracy alone does not. Two models can lose the same
amount of accuracy under corruption yet differ in how much of that loss
comes from information destruction versus persistent reliance on an
unhelpful input. The swapped-image condition, which replaces information
with misinformation, consistently produces higher terminal reliance than
Gaussian noise, and this gap is statistically significant for all three
models. The gap means that the model's output distribution remains
further from its no-image behaviour when given a wrong image than when
given noise, even though both are uninformative for the answer in most cases.

\paragraph{Baseline sensitivity.}
Conclusions for individual models depend on the no-input baseline. The
black-image and removed-image predictions differ little for Qwen2-VL and
substantially for SmolVLM and LLaVA-1.5: as an informal heuristic (JSD is
not additive), this divergence equals about
\checknum{3.6}{floor:Qwen2-VL-2B-Instruct:floor_pct}\%,
\checknum{43}{floor:SmolVLM-Instruct:floor_pct}\% and
\checknum{42}{floor:llava-1.5-7b-hf:floor_pct}\% of swapped-image terminal
reliance for the three models. We hypothesize, without testing it, that part
of the reliance of SmolVLM and LLaVA-1.5 reflects the presence of image
tokens and not their content. The black-image baseline, which keeps the
token structure, gives lower values, but we do not claim it is more accurate.

\paragraph{Implications for evaluation.}
The pre-declared criterion was met in only two of 42 combinations.
This does not mean over-reliance is absent; it means that a strict,
pre-specified criterion is hard to satisfy when both reliance and utility
carry sampling uncertainty and when conclusions shift with the baseline.
Our results suggest that studies comparing predictions with and without
image tokens may overestimate visual dependence when the token structure itself
shifts predictions, and we recommend that future evaluations report results
under at least two baselines and state how the no-input condition is constructed.

\paragraph{Dropout and raw versus normalized reliance.}
In the fusion proxy, dropout lowers raw terminal reliance and raises
accuracy with view A removed or degraded, but terminal utility remains
negative for every $p$ and is not monotone in $p$. The normalized measures
$\hat R$ and MI move in the opposite direction; we suggest (post hoc) that
this reflects the shrinking denominator $R(0)$. Ratio-based measures should
be read with caution when clean-input reliance differs across models.
