\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We proposed a severity-indexed diagnostic that separates information loss
from lingering reliance by pairing utility and distributional reliance along
a degradation path. Controlled synthetic experiments show that the measures
order the architectures as intended, although the first success criterion
(Gated MI below 0.2) was not met. On three open VLMs, swapped-image
corruptions leave normalized reliance at 0.49--0.58 of its clean level and
produce a utility inversion for one model; both conclusions depend on the
choice of no-input baseline, and a strict pre-declared over-reliance
criterion is met in only \checknum{2}{criterion:status:count_met} of 42
evaluated combinations. The diagnostic is black-box, applicable to any
multimodal system, and its reproducibility pipeline is openly available.
