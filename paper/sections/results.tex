\section{Empirical Evaluation on Vision-Language Models}
\label{sec:results}

We evaluate reliance-utility alignment on open-weights Vision-Language Models evaluated on the A-OKVQA benchmark ($N=500$ disjoint validation items):
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Qwen2-VL-2B-Instruct} (native resolution vision transformer)
    \item \textbf{SmolVLM-Instruct} (compact vision-language model)
    \item \textbf{LLaVA-1.5-7B-hf} (4-bit NF4 quantized instruction model)
\end{itemize}
Each model is evaluated across three corruption types: \emph{Gaussian blur/noise blend}, \emph{random patch occlusion}, and \emph{deceptive visual swap-blend}.
